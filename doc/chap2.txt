  [33X[0;0YThis chapter lists all functions contained in the [5XIdPGroup[105X package.[133X
  
  
  [1X2.1 [33X[0;0YIdentifying groups via a search tree[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  section  briefly describes the functions and variables of this package
  associated  with the identification of an arbitrary group of order 512 via a
  search tree. For further informations see [ES26].[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YBy  'identify'  a  group  [22XG[122X,  we  mean  that we want to find an [13Xid[113X such that
  [10XSmallGroup(512,  id)[110X  is  isomorphic  to  [22XG[122X.  This  is  done  by calculating
  invariants  and  comparing  them  with the ones of the remaining candidates.
  This  process  is  organized in the tree [2XID_GROUP_512_TREE[102X ([14X2.1-3[114X). The tree
  used is implemented as nested records with the following components:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"fp": a list of integers, each being the encoded fingerprint of groups
        resulting from the specific test performed at the current level of the
        identification routine.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"next":  a  list comprised of the respective subbranches corresponding
        to  the fingerprints in "fp". This is either a list of ids or a branch
        implemented as a record itself.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YDepending  on  the  depth  in  the  tree,  each  record  also has one of the
  following two components:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"test":  a  string  that indicates which test was used via [2XTestOnGroup[102X
        ([14X2.4-3[114X) to create the listed fingerprints.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"desc":  a list of integers that encode a description of the performed
        powermap-test.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  first  levels of [2XID_GROUP_512_TREE[102X ([14X2.1-3[114X) try to distinguish groups by
  the following invariants:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe rank of the group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe collected orders of all elements of the group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  numbers  of  conjugacy  classes of each length and representative
        order (the order of the elements in the class) of the group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  isomorphism type of the parent of the group, i.e. the quotient of
        the  group and the smallest non-trivial element of the lower-[23Xp[123X-central
        series,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  abelian  invariants,  i.e.  the  isomorphism  types of the factor
        groups of the derived series of the group [22XG[122X and[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  cycle  structure  of  the permutations induced on all clusters of
        conjugacy classes of same length and representative order by the power
        map [23Xx\mapsto x^3[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIf  necessary,  these  invariants  are followed by invariants resulting from
  power  maps  evaluated  on partitions of [22XG[122X. The initial partition is made of
  the  conjugacy  classes of [22XG[122X grouped such that conjugacy classes of the same
  length and same representative order belong to the same partitioning subset.
  The  collected  result of a power map applied to a subset forms an invariant
  used  to  distinguish  groups  in  these  levels  of  the  search  tree. The
  describing  parameters  (e.g.  which subset and power map) are determined by
  the  content of the component "desc". After the evaluation, the partition is
  refined  according  to the respective result. If the preceding invariants do
  not  lead  to a single group, the following (more time consuming) invariants
  are calculated:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  isomorphism  types  of  maximal  factors  formed by factoring out
        subgroups  generated  by elements of the smallest non-trivial subgroup
        in the lower-p-central series,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  isomorphism  types  of  maximal  factors  formed by the group and
        central subgroups and[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe isomorphism types of all maximal subgroups.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YAfter  this  process, 139 pairs and 1 triplet of groups remain unidentified.
  These  groups  are  distinguished by a random isomorphism test, the same way
  ultimately  used  in  the [5XSmallGrps[105X library for the groups of order 256, for
  more details see [BE99].[133X

  [1X2.2 [33X[0;0YLoading (strong) siblings[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  section  describes  the  functions used to load precalculated lists of
  (strong) siblings, i.e. groups satisying the following definitions:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xsubgroup-equivalent  groups[113X  are  groups  with a bijection between the
        sets of proper subgroups s.t. preimage and image are isomorphic,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xfactor-equivalent  groups[113X are groups with a bijection between the sets
        of proper factors s.t. preimage and image are isomorphic,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xsiblings[113X   are  subgroup-  and  factor-equivalent  groups,  where  the
        bijection  of  proper  subgroups  preserves  conjugacy  and  maps  the
        Frattini  subgroup, lower and upper central series onto the respective
        counterparts,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xstrong siblings[113X are siblings that are also Brauer Pairs.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YAll  (strong)  siblings  of  orders  dividing 512 together with some trivial
  cases  are  implemented  in  this package, and are callable by the following
  functions.[133X
  
  [1X2.2-1 Siblings[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XSiblings[102X( [3Xorder[103X, [3Xjustids[103X[, [3Xindices[103X] ) [32X function[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10YA  list  of lists of siblings of size [3Xorder[103X. The parameter [3Xjustids[103X
            determines  wether  the  ids  or  the  groups  (default)  shall be
            returned.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIf  available, loads and returns a list of siblings of size [3Xorder[103X. Currently
  available sizes are:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall orders of the form [23Xp\,q[123X for [23Xp[123X and [23Xq[123X primes,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall powers of 2 up to [23X2^9[123X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall powers of 3 up to [23X3^6[123X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall powers of a prime up to [23Xp^4[123X and[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall  orders  smaller  than 256 except 60, 120, 160, 168, 180, 189, 192
        and 240.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIf  no list is available for the provided order, [10Xfail[110X is returned. If a list
  [3Xindices[103X is given, only the respective entries are returned.[133X

    [4X[28X    <pc group of size 128 with 7 generators> ],[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  [ <pc group of size 128 with 7 generators>,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X    <pc group of size 128 with 7 generators> ],[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  [ <pc group of size 128 with 7 generators>,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X    <pc group of size 128 with 7 generators> ] ][128X[104X

  [1X2.4 [33X[0;0YFunctions for building a search tree[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  section  describes all functions and variables connected to building a
  search  tree that can be used with [2XIdGroup512[102X ([14X2.1-2[114X) to identify groups via
  implemented invariants.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIn  short, this is done by initializing a tree via calling [2XTestOnClusterList[102X
  ([14X2.4-4[114X)  with  a selected test on a list of ids of groups of given order and
  then iterating the following three steps:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ycollecting the clusters of the current tree via [2XClusterList[102X ([14X2.4-2[114X),[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yperforming  a  chosen  test  on  these  clusters via [2XTestOnClusterList[102X
        ([14X2.4-4[114X) and[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yextending  the  tree with these test results via [2XAddClusterTestResults[102X
        ([14X2.4-5[114X).[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YA   very   effective   set   of   invariants  can  be  obtained  by  calling
  [2XCocTestOnClusterList[102X  ([14X2.4-7[114X)  in  the second step. This should only be done
  when  the groups of each provided cluster yield the same value when the test
  [10XCocList[110X  is  applied. Furthermore these invariants should be used for as few
  as  possible  clusters  since [2XCocTestOnClusterList[102X ([14X2.4-7[114X) includes a search
  for suitable parameters that can take quite some time.[133X
  
  [1X2.4-1 InfoTreeBuild[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XInfoTreeBuild[102X [32X info class[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YInfo class for the building of a search tree.[133X
  
  [1X2.4-2 ClusterList[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XClusterList[102X( [3Xtree[103X ) [32X function[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10YA  list  of  all  clusters  of  groups in [3Xtree[103X (i.e. the leaves of
            [3Xtree[103X).[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIterates  through [3Xtree[103X and collects information about each leaf as a record.
  This  includes  the  size  of the leaf, the path through [3Xtree[103X leading to the
  leaf,  the  tests  performed  including  descriptions of performed power map
  tests and the ids of the groups contained in the leaf.[133X
  
  [4X[32X  Example  [32X[104X
    [4X[25Xgap>[125X [27Xtree := rec( fp := [ 63049, 75721, 4310 ],  next := [ [ 3, 8 ], [ 4 ],[127X[104X

    [4X[25Xgap>[125X [27XClusterList(tree);[127X[104X
    [4X[28X[ rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 3, 8 ], path := [ 63049 ], size := 2,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds" ] ),[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 4 ], path := [ 75721 ], size := 1,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds" ] ),[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 5 ], path := [ 4310, 11744 ], size := 1,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds", "MaxSubId" ] ),[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 6, 7 ], path := [ 4310, 86646 ], size := 2,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds", "MaxSubId" ] ) ][128X[104X

  [1X2.4-3 TestOnGroup[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XTestOnGroup[102X( [3XG[103X, [3Xtest[103X[, [3Xopt[103X] ) [32X function[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10YThe result of [3Xtest[103X applied to [3XG[103X, if desired encoded as an integer.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YCalculates  an  invariant of the group [3XG[103X. The following choices for [3Xtest[103X are
  implemented:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"ElmOrds"[103X: the collected orders of the elements of [3XG[103X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"Center"[103X: the abelian invariants of the center of [3XG[103X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"Rank"[103X: the rank of [3XG[103X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"AbelInv"[103X:  the  order  and  abelian  invariants  of  each term in the
        derived series of [3XG[103X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"UCSId"[103X:  the  isomorphism  types of the proper subgroups in the upper
        central series of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"LCSId"[103X:  the  isomorphism  types of the proper subgroups in the lower
        central series of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"PCSId"[103X:  the  isomorphism  types  of  the  proper  subgroups  in  the
        p-central series of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"SpSubId"[103X: the isomorphismtypes of the Frattini subgroup, the terms in
        the #! upper and lower central series of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"MaxSubId"[103X:  the  collected isomorphism types of all maximal subgroups
        of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"SubId"[103X:  the  sizes  and  collected  isomorphism  types  of conjugacy
        classes of all proper subgroups via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"MaxFactId"[103X: the collected isomorphism types of all maximal factors of
        [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"CentQuot"[103X: the collected isomorphism types of all quotients of [3XG[103X with
        subgroups generated by non-trivial central elements of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"LCSFactId"[103X:  the  collected  isomorphism  types of all quotients of [3XG[103X
        with  cyclic  subgroups  generated by non-trivial elements of the last
        non-trivial term of the [23Xp[123X-central series,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"FactId"[103X:  the  collected isomorphism types of all proper factors of [3XG[103X
        via [10XIdGroup[110X,[133X
