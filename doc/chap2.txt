  [33X[0;0YThis chapter lists all functions contained in the [5XIdPGroup[105X package.[133X
  
  
  [1X2.1 [33X[0;0YIdentifying groups via a search tree[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  section  briefly describes the functions and variables of this package
  associated  to  the  identification of an arbitrary group of order 512 via a
  search  tree.  By  'identify' a group [22XG[122X we mean that we want to find [13Xid[113X such
  that  [10XSmallGroup(512,  id)[110X  is  isomorphic to [22XG[122X. This is done by calculating
  invariants  and  comparing  them  with the ones of the remaining candidates.
  This process is organized in the tree [2XID_GROUP_512_TREE[102X ([14X2.1-3[114X).[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe   used  tree  is  implemented  as  nested  records  with  the  following
  components:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"fp": a list of integers, each being the encoded fingerprint of groups
        resulting from the specific test performed at the current level of the
        identification routine.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"next":  a list comprised of the resprective subbranches corresponding
        to  the fingerprints in "fp". This is either a list of ids or a branch
        implemented as a record itself.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YDepending on the depth in the tree each record also has one of the following
  two components:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"test":  a  string  that indicates which test was used via [2XTestOnGroup[102X
        ([14X2.4-2[114X) to create the listed fingerprints.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y"desc":  a list of integers that encode a description of the performed
        powermap-test[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  first  levels of [2XID_GROUP_512_TREE[102X ([14X2.1-3[114X) try to distinguish groups by
  the following invariants:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe rank of the group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe collected orders of all elements of the group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  numbers  of  conjugacy  classes of each length and representative
        order (the order of the elements in the class) of the group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  isomorphism type of the parent of the group, i.e. the quotient of
        the  group and the smallest non-trivial element of the lower-[23Xp[123X-central
        series,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  abelian  invarants,  i.e.  the isomorphism type of the commutator
        factor group of the group [22XG[122X and[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  cycle  structure  of  the permutations induced on all clusters of
        conjugacy classes of same length and representative order by the power
        map [23Xx\mapsto x^3[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIf  necessary  these  invariants  are  followed by invaraints resulting from
  power  maps  evaluated  on partitions of [22XG[122X. The initial partition is made of
  the  conjugacy  classes of [22XG[122X grouped such that conjugacy classes of the same
  length and same representative order belong to the same partitioning subset.
  The  collected  result  of a power map applied to a subset forms a invaraint
  used  to  distinguish  groups  in  these  levels  of  the  search  tree. The
  describing  parameters (e.g. which subset, which power map) is determined by
  the  content of the component "desc". After the evaluation, the partition is
  refined according to the respective result.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIf  the  preceding  invaraints  do not lead to a single group, the following
  (more time consuming) invaraints are calculated.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6YThe  isomorphism  types of the non-trivial factors formed by the group
        and   elements   of   the   smallest   non-trivial   element   of  the
        lower-p-central series,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe  isomorphism  types of the non-trivial factors formed by the group
        and elements of the center and[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ythe isomorphism types of all maximal subgroups.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YRemaining  are 139 pairs and 1 triplet of groups of unidentified groups. All
  these  groups are distinguished by a random isomorphism test the same way as
  groups of order 256 are finally distinguished in the [5XSmallGrps[105X library.[133X

  [1X2.2 [33X[0;0YLoading (strong) siblings[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  section  describes  the  functions used to load precalculated lists of
  (strong) siblings, i.e. groups that fulfil the following definitions:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xsubgroup-equivalent  groups[113X  are  groups  with a bijection between the
        sets of proper subgroups s.t. preimage and image are isomorphic,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xfactor-equivalent  groups[113X are groups with a bijection between the sets
        of proper factors s.t. preimage and image are isomorphic,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xsiblings[113X   are  subgroup-  and  factor-equivalent  groups,  where  the
        bijection of proper subgroups preservs conjugacy and maps the Frattini
        subgroup,   lower   and  upper  central  series  onto  the  respective
        counterparts,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[13Xstrong siblings[113X are siblings that are also Brauer Pairs.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YAll  (strong)  siblings  of  orders  dividing 512 together with some trivial
  cases  are  implemented  in  this  package and are callable by the following
  functions.[133X
  
  [1X2.2-1 Siblings[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XSiblings[102X( [3Xorder[103X, [3Xjustids[103X[, [3Xindices[103X] ) [32X function[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10YA  list  of lists of siblings of size [3Xorder[103X. The parameter [3Xjustids[103X
            controls  if  just  the  ids  or  the  groups  (default)  shall be
            returned.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YLoads  and  returns a list of siblings of size [3Xorder[103X if available. Currently
  available sizes are:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall orders of the form [23Xp\,q[123X for [23Xp[123X and [23Xq[123X primes,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall powers of 2 smaller than [23X2^9[123X[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall powers of 3 smaller than [23X3^5[123X[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yall powers of a prime smaller than [23Xp^4[123X and[133X

  [1X2.4 [33X[0;0YFunctions for building a search tree[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  section  describes all functions and variables connected to building a
  search  tree that can be used with [2XIdGroup512[102X ([14X2.1-2[114X) to identify groups via
  implemented invariants.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIn  short  this is done be initializing a tree via calling [2XTestOnClusterList[102X
  ([14X2.4-3[114X)  with a selected test on a list of (lists of) ids of groups of given
  order and then iteratnig the following three steps[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6YCollecting the clusters of the curent tree via [2XClusterList[102X ([14X2.4-1[114X),[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6YPerforming  a  choosen  test  on  these clusters via [2XTestOnClusterList[102X
        ([14X2.4-3[114X),[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6YExtending  the  tree with these test results via [2XAddClusterTestResults[102X
        ([14X2.4-4[114X).[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YA   very   effective   set   of   invaraints  can  be  obtained  by  calling
  [2XCocTestOnClusterList[102X  ([14X2.4-6[114X)  in  the second step. This should only be done
  when the groups of each provided cluster yields the same value when the test
  [10XCocList[110X  is  applied. Furthermore these invaraints should be used for as few
  as  possible  clusters  since [2XCocTestOnClusterList[102X ([14X2.4-6[114X) includes a search
  for suitable parameters than can take quite some time.[133X
  
  [1X2.4-1 ClusterList[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XClusterList[102X( [3Xtree[103X ) [32X function[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10YA list of all clusters of groups in [3Xtree[103X (i.e. the leafs of [3Xtree[103X).[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIterates  through [3Xtree[103X and collects information about each leaf as a record.
  This  includes  the  size  of the leaf, the path through [3Xtree[103X leading to the
  leaf, the tests performed including descriptions of performed powermap-tests
  and the ids of the groups contained in the leaf.[133X
  
  [4X[32X  Example  [32X[104X
    [4X[25Xgap>[125X [27Xtree := rec( fp := [ 63049, 75721, 4310 ],  next := [ [ 3, 8 ], [ 4 ],[127X[104X
    [4X[25Xgap>[125X [27X           rec( fp := [ 11744, 86646 ], next := [ [ 5 ], [ 6, 7 ] ],[127X[104X
    [4X[25Xgap>[125X [27X               test := "MaxSubId" ) ], test := "ElmOrds" );;[127X[104X
    [4X[25Xgap>[125X [27XClusterList(tree);[127X[104X
    [4X[28X[ rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 3, 8 ], path := [ 63049 ], size := 2,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds" ] ),[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 4 ], path := [ 75721 ], size := 1,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds" ] ),[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 5 ], path := [ 4310, 11744 ], size := 1,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds", "MaxSubId" ] ),[128X[104X
    [4X[28X  rec( descl := [  ], ids := [ 6, 7 ], path := [ 4310, 86646 ], size := 2,[128X[104X
    [4X[28X       tests := [ "ElmOrds", "MaxSubId" ] ) ][128X[104X

  [1X2.4-2 TestOnGroup[101X
  
  [33X[1;0Y[29X[2XTestOnGroup[102X( [3XG[103X, [3Xtest[103X[, [3Xopt[103X] ) [32X function[133X
  [6XReturns:[106X  [33X[0;10YThe result of [3Xtest[103X applied to [3XG[103X, if desried encoded as an integer.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YPerforms  the  selected  [3Xtest[103X on the group [3XG[103X. The following choices for [3Xtest[103X
  are implemented:[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"ElmOrds"[103X: Collects the orders of all elements of [3XG[103X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"Center"[103X: Calculates the abelian invariants of the center of [3XG[103X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"Rank"[103X: Calculates the rank of [3XG[103X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"AbelInv"[103X:  Computes  the order of each term of the derived series and
        the abelian Invariants of the derived factors of [3XG[103X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"UCSId"[103X:  Identifies  the proper subgroups of the upper central series
        of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"LCSId"[103X:  Identifies  the proper subgroups of the lower central series
        of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"PCSId"[103X:  Identifies the proper subgroups of the p-central series of [3XG[103X
        via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"SpSubId"[103X: Computes the Frattini subgroup, the upper and lower central
        series of [3XG[103X and identifies all terms if possible via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"MaxSubId"[103X:  Collects  the  sizes and isomorphism types of all maximal
        subgroups of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"SubId"[103X:  Collects  the  sizes  and  isormorphism  types  of conjugacy
        classes of all proper subgoups via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"MaxFactId"[103X:  Collects  the sizes and isomorphism types of all maximal
        factors of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"CentQuot"[103X:  Collects the sizes and isomorphism types of all quotients
        of [3XG[103X with non-trivial elements of the center of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"LCSFactId"[103X: Collects the isomorphism types of all quotients of [3XG[103X with
        with cyclic subgroups generated by by non-trivial elements of the last
        non-trivial term of the p-central series.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"FactId"[103X: Identifies all proper factors of [3XG[103X via [10XIdGroup[110X.[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[3X"CocList"[103X:  Collects  the  number  of conjugacy classes of a [3XG[103X of same
